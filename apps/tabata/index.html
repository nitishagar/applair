What it does

A simple, reliable interval timer for high-intensity training — built for HIIT athletes, trainers and anyone who wants a no-nonsense workout timer.

  • Tabata: prepare, work, rest, rounds, cycles, rest-between-cycles and cooldown
  • Rounds mode for boxing, martial arts and circuits
  • Stopwatch with prepare countdown, laps and interval beeps
  • Workout history with progress stats, stored on-device
  • Countdown, phase-change and completion sound cues
  • Landscape mode for gym display

Screens

Under the hood

  • Background-safe timer driver — keeps running when the app is minimized
  • Redux-persisted settings with one-shot history migration
  • Color-coded phases and wake lock for glanceable gym use
  • Analytics strictly opt-in and off by default; tests fully mocked, no network calls
  • Deterministic timer test helpers for a green, flake-free suite

Privacy

By default the app collects nothing — history, settings and feedback stay on the device. Read the privacy policy and the data-safety declaration.
