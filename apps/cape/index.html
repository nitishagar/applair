What it does

Tell cape your starting daily intake and it builds a gradual, week-by-week taper plan with steady plateaus — then helps you follow it, one day at a time.

  • Personalized taper plans (2, 4 or 8 weeks) with daily targets
  • Daily check-ins with optional notes
  • Craving log with intensity tracking
  • Streaks, XP, achievements and milestones
  • Guided breathing for tough moments
  • Optional daily reminder, fully on-device
  • Hold-a-week option when life gets in the way
  • Local data export and in-app delete-all-data

Screens

Under the hood

  • Piecewise-linear taper math with weekly checkpoints, unit-tested
  • Drift/SQLite persistence with Riverpod state management
  • Material 3 light/dark themes, WCAG-aware accent palette
  • Age gate (18+) and health disclosure on first launch
  • CI release gates: analyze, tests, permission and no-network checks

Not a medical device. cape is an educational self-tracking tool and makes no treatment claims — the listing, age gate and disclosures all say so consistently.

Privacy

Everything entered stays on the phone: no backend, no accounts, no analytics or crash SDK. Read the privacy policy and the data-safety declaration.
