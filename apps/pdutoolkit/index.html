What it does

An offline power companion for data-centre and electrical technicians, colo engineers and students. Every result shows its formula and assumptions.

  • kW / kVA / amps for single-phase and three-phase (line-to-line and line-to-neutral)
  • PUE and BTU / kW / tons conversions
  • PDU phase balance with imbalance percentage and derating-basis control
  • UPS runtime estimates with inputs shown and limits disclaimed
  • Saved rack power budgets on-device, with totals
  • PDF and CSV export through the share sheet

Screens

Under the hood

  • Pure, unit-tested power math — voltage basis is part of the API, never a hidden assumption
  • Overflow/NaN-safe display rules; invalid input shows a message, never a bad number
  • Fully offline: no network SDK of any kind, enforced by CI checks on every push
  • Zero Android permissions; first-run disclaimer gate
  • Data-safety contract kept in lock-step with the Play declaration

Privacy

Inputs, budgets and exports never leave the device except through a share the user initiates. Read the privacy policy and the data-safety declaration.
