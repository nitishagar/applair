DualUnit — Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

DualUnit is a Shopify app that renders market-adaptive unit prices on merchants' storefronts: the statutory EU base price (Grundpreis) for EU shoppers and per-oz / per-lb shelf-style pricing for US shoppers.

Summary

DualUnit collects no shopper data and shares no data. Unit-price math runs entirely in the shopper's browser. From merchants we store only what the app needs to operate: store domain, plan state, and display preferences. Nominal quantities merchants enter are stored as metafields on their own Shopify products — not in our database.

Data we collect

CategoryWhatWhere it livesShared?
Store information Store domain, plan tier, display preferences Encrypted PostgreSQL (Neon) + AWS us-east-1 No
Catalog identifiers Product/variant IDs, titles, prices — read only for the Unit Specs manager Fetched live via the Shopify Admin API; not retained beyond the session No
Session tokens Shopify session tokens via Shopify's official session storage Encrypted database; used only to call Shopify APIs on the merchant's behalf No

We do not collect: shopper personal data, orders, browsing behavior, identifiers, email, location, or any advertising ID.

How we use it

  • Listing products/variants in the Unit Specs manager.
  • Saving nominal quantities to the merchant's product metafields.
  • Enforcing plan limits and showing plan status in the admin dashboard.

Third parties

Infrastructure providers (AWS, Neon) process data only as necessary to operate the service, as data processors under our direction. We do not sell, rent, or share data with anyone else.

Retention

  • While installed — settings and plan state for the subscription duration.
  • After uninstall — automatic deletion once Shopify sends the data-erasure request. Unit-spec metafields stay on the merchant's products until the merchant removes them.

Your rights

Merchants may request access, correction, deletion, or portability of their data at any time under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws. Uninstalling the app triggers automatic deletion.

Cookies and tracking

No cookies, analytics, or third-party trackers. Admin auth uses Shopify session tokens.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be noted in the Last updated line at the top of this page.
