Rack Power Toolkit — Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

Rack Power Toolkit is an offline rack-PDU power calculator for data-centre and electrical technicians, colo engineers, and students.

Summary

Rack Power Toolkit stores every piece of personal data on your device. Nothing you enter — power inputs and results, saved rack budgets, or generated exports — is transmitted off the device. The app has no backend server, no account system, requests zero Android permissions, and does not use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Data we collect

CategoryWhatWhere it livesShared?
App activity App interactions: power inputs you type, saved rack power budgets, disclaimer acceptance flag Device-local SQLite database (Android internal storage) and SharedPreferences No
Files and docs budget files: CSV and PDF exports you generate from a saved budget App-private cache; handed to the Android share sheet only when you tap share No — shared only at your explicit action

We do not collect: identifiers, email, location, contacts, messages, photos, audio, or any advertising ID.

How data is used

Data is used only to compute and render your own power results, saved budgets, and exports. It never leaves the device except through a share you initiate yourself.

Third parties

Rack Power Toolkit uses the following open-source libraries. None of them transmit your data off the device in this app's configuration:

  • Drift / sqlite3 — local database
  • shared_preferences — local key/value storage
  • pdf — generates export files on-device (no network)
  • share_plus — hands your export to the Android share sheet
  • fl_chart, go_router, Riverpod — UI only (no network)

The app does not integrate with any ads SDK, tracking SDK, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK.

Permissions

Rack Power Toolkit requests no Android permissions — not even network access. The app is fully offline.

Your controls

  • Delete all data — uninstall the app. Android removes all of the app's private storage on uninstall (backup is disabled).
  • Export — each saved budget offers PDF and CSV export through the Android share sheet; the files are yours to keep.

Not professional electrical advice

Rack Power Toolkit is a calculation aid. It is not a substitute for professional electrical judgement: every result shows its formula and assumptions — verify them against your nameplate ratings and local electrical codes before acting on them. UPS runtimes are estimates only.

Children

Rack Power Toolkit is a general-audience professional utility. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be noted in the Last updated line at the top of this page and called out in the app's release notes for the relevant version.
