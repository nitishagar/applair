BillSplit — Google Play Data Safety Declaration

This page is the source of truth for the Data Safety section of the BillSplit Play Store listing. Copy the values below into the Play Console when prompted.

Data collection & sharing summary

QuestionAnswer
Does the app collect or share any user data?Yes (on-device processing; sharing limited to Google Play purchase processing)
Is all of the user data collected by the app encrypted in transit?Yes — on-device data never transmits; the Play Billing purchase uses Google's encrypted channel
Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted?Yes — uninstalling the app removes all app-local data

Security practices

  • Data is encrypted in transit — Yes (on-device data never transmits; purchases use Google's encrypted channel)
  • You can request data be deleted — users can uninstall to remove all data
  • Committed to Play Families Policy — N/A (general finance utility, no children's content)
  • Independent security review — No (solo developer)

Third-party SDKs

BillSplit integrates no analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs. Google Play services ML Kit (on-device OCR) and Google Play Billing (one-time purchase) run under Google's privacy policy.
