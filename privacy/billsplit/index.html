BillSplit — Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

BillSplit (com.nitishagar.billsplit) is an offline-first optical receipt scanner and bill splitter for Android.

Summary

Your receipts never leave your phone. Capture, text recognition, parsing, splitting math, and export rendering all run on-device — including in airplane mode. BillSplit has no account system and uses no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDK. The only data that ever leaves the device is the optional one-time SplitPro purchase, which is processed by Google Play (see below).

Data we collect

We do not collect: identifiers, email, location, contacts, messages, audio, browsing history, or any advertising ID.

Receipt images and export cards are shared externally only when you tap Share — through Android's share sheet, to the app you choose.

How data is used

Data is used only to scan your receipts, compute your splits, and render your exports — all on your device. UPI IDs are used solely to build upi://pay links and QR codes locally; BillSplit never initiates, processes, or observes any payment.

Third parties

  • Google Play services — ML Kit (Document Scanner, Text Recognition). Capture cleanup and OCR run on-device. BillSplit requests no camera permission; scanning runs inside the Play services flow, under Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing. Processes the optional SplitPro one-time purchase, under Google's privacy policy. No other purchase or payment data touches our code.
  • On-device libraries only otherwise — Jetpack Room (storage), ZXing (QR generation), Jetpack Compose UI. None of them transmit data.

BillSplit does not integrate with any ads SDK, tracking SDK, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK.

Permissions

BillSplit requests no runtime permissions — no camera, no location, no contacts, no storage, no internet access for its core features.

Your controls

  • Delete all data — uninstall the app. Android removes all of BillSplit's private storage on uninstall.
  • Receipt photos — clearing the app's cache removes any cached scan images.

Children

BillSplit is a general finance utility with no age-restricted content. Since the app collects no personal data beyond what is listed above (all on-device), there is nothing to delete beyond uninstalling.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be noted in the Last updated line at the top of this page and called out in the app's release notes for the relevant version.
