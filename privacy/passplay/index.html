PassPlay — Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

PassPlay is a single-device pass-and-play social deduction game for 3–12 players.

Summary

PassPlay collects no data and shares no data. Player names exist only in memory while a game runs and vanish when it ends. The app has no backend server, no account system, no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDK, and requests zero Android permissions. It works fully offline, including in airplane mode.

Data we collect

None. Specifically:

CategoryWhatWhere it livesShared?
Player names Nicknames typed at setup (e.g. "Asha") RAM only — never written to storage, gone when the game ends No
App preferences Sound on/off toggle Android DataStore (app-private storage) No

We do not collect: identifiers, email, location, contacts, messages, photos, audio, health data, financial data, or any advertising ID.

Sharing (you initiate it)

Deck links, QR codes, and scoreboard images leave the phone only when you tap a share button, through the Android share sheet or clipboard — you pick the destination every time. The app never uploads anything by itself.

Third parties

PassPlay uses the following open-source libraries. None of them transmit your data off the device:

  • Jetpack Compose / Activity / Lifecycle / DataStore — UI and the sound setting
  • ZXing core — on-device QR code rendering

PassPlay does not integrate with any ads SDK, tracking SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, or billing SDK.

Permissions

PassPlay declares zero Android permissions.

Your controls

  • Delete everything — uninstall the app. Android removes the sound setting; names were never stored.
  • Sound — toggle in setup.

Children

PassPlay is a party game for teens and adults and is not designed for children. It collects no data from anyone.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be noted in the Last updated line at the top of this page and called out in the app's release notes for the relevant version.

Contact
