PriceAnchor — Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-19

PriceAnchor is a Shopify app that tracks merchants' own price history to render statutory reference prices on their storefronts: the lowest price of the prior 30 days for EU shoppers (EU Omnibus) and date-disclosed former-price framing for US shoppers.

Summary

PriceAnchor collects no shopper data and shares no data. The storefront line renders from price metadata on the merchant's own products. From merchants we store only what the app needs to operate: store domain, plan state, guard preferences, and the per-variant price history that forms the audit trail.

Data we collect

CategoryWhatWhere it livesShared?
Store information Store domain, plan tier, stale-guard preferences Encrypted PostgreSQL (Neon); app runs on Cloudflare Workers No
Price history Variant prices and compare-at values with timestamps, per market and currency Encrypted PostgreSQL (Neon); computed floors mirrored to the merchant's product metafields No
Session tokens Shopify session tokens via Shopify's official session storage Encrypted database; used only to call Shopify APIs on the merchant's behalf No

We do not collect: shopper personal data, orders, browsing behavior, identifiers, email, location, or any advertising ID.

How we use it

  • Recording price changes from product webhooks into the history ledger.
  • Computing the EU 30-day lowest prior price and the US 90-day former price.
  • Flagging (or, when enabled, auto-clearing) stale compare-at prices.
  • Enforcing plan limits and showing plan status in the admin dashboard.

Third parties

Infrastructure providers (Cloudflare, Neon) process data only as necessary to operate the service, as data processors under our direction. We do not sell, rent, or share data with anyone else.

Retention

  • While installed — price history for the subscription duration (the audit trail requires it).
  • After uninstall — automatic deletion once Shopify sends the data-erasure request. Reference-price metafields stay on the merchant's products until the merchant removes them.

Your rights

Merchants may request access, correction, deletion, or portability of their data at any time under GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and similar laws. Uninstalling the app triggers automatic deletion.

Cookies and tracking

No cookies, analytics, or third-party trackers. Admin auth uses Shopify session tokens.
