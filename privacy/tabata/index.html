T-timer — Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-07

T-timer (Tabata Timer, package com.nitishagar.tabatatimer) is a HIIT interval timer for Android. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and your rights.

Summary

By default T-timer collects nothing. Workout history, timer settings, and feedback entries stay on your device and are never transmitted. Only if you explicitly opt in to analytics does the app send anonymous usage events to Firebase Analytics (Google LLC). There are no ads, no accounts, and no backend server.

Data we collect (only with your consent)

If you enable analytics at first launch (or later via More → Analytics), the app sends the following anonymous events to Firebase Analytics:

  • Workout started / completed (duration, number of rounds and cycles)
  • Phase changes (work, rest, prepare)
  • Settings updates (interval durations, rounds, cycles — no personal values)
  • Feedback opened / submitted (event counts only — never the message text or email)

No personal information is collected. No name, email address, device ID, precise location, or health data is ever collected or transmitted.

Local-only data

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted:

  • Workout history (dates and durations of completed workouts)
  • Timer settings / preferences
  • Feedback entries, including any optional email address you type (there is no feedback backend in v1; entries never leave your phone)

How data is used

On-device data is used only to render your own workout history and remember your timer preferences. Opt-in analytics events are used only to understand aggregate usage (e.g. which timers are popular) and improve the app.

Third parties

ServicePurposeActivated
Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) Anonymous usage analytics Only when you opt in

Firebase Analytics privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

T-timer does not integrate with any ads SDK, tracking SDK, or crash-reporting SDK.

Permissions

  • INTERNET — to send opt-in analytics events only. The timer works fully offline.
  • WAKE_LOCK / foreground service — to keep the timer running while the screen is off.

No other permission is requested. The app does not request location, contacts, storage, microphone, or camera access.

Advertising

There are no advertisements in T-timer.

Data retention

  • Analytics events are retained by Firebase for up to 14 months per Google's default policy.
  • Local data (workout history, preferences) is retained until you clear it or uninstall the app.

Deleting your data

  • Workout history: More → Workout History → Clear History.
  • All local data: uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
  • Analytics opt-out: More → Analytics toggle (off). This disables collection immediately and stops all future data collection.

Children

T-timer is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be noted in the Last updated line at the top of this page and called out in the app's release notes for the relevant version.
