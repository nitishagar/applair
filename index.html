Approach

How we think.

Boring technology, used well

We reach for the plain tool first. Native platform primitives, well-known libraries, and patterns that survive a team handoff.

Tight feedback loops

Fast builds, fast tests, fast store releases. The loop between "I think this works" and "a user confirmed it works" is the real product.

Ship to learn

Smaller releases, observed in the wild, beat bigger releases guessed at in a sprint-planning doc. Every engagement ends with something shipped.
